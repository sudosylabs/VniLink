# VniLink code of conduct

## Our commitment

VniLink welcomes participants of every background and level of experience.
Everyone acting in good faith should be able to contribute without harassment
or discrimination based on race, ethnicity, caste, age, disability, gender,
gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, religion, nationality,
language, socioeconomic position, education, or other personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Treat other people with respect and consider differences in culture,
  experience, language, and communication style.
- Give useful, constructive feedback about the work. Disagreement is welcome;
  personal attacks are not.
- Respect boundaries, privacy, and requests to stop an interaction.
- Credit other people's work and present your own contributions honestly.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes, listen to feedback, and repair harm.

Harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing,
impersonation, and retaliation against a reporter are not acceptable. Neither
are deliberate evasion of moderation or promotion of these behaviors. Respect
the purpose of project spaces and avoid unrelated promotional material.

## Scope

This policy applies to VniLink repositories, issues, pull requests, reviews,
project communications, and official community events. It also applies when
someone represents VniLink through an official account or appointed role.

## Report an incident

Email [support@sudosy.fr](mailto:support@sudosy.fr?subject=%5BVniLink%20Code%20of%20Conduct%5D)
with the subject prefix `[VniLink Code of Conduct]`. Include relevant links,
dates, context, and any immediate safety concern, sharing only evidence you
are comfortable providing. Do not post private incident details publicly.

If the report concerns someone with access to that mailbox, contact a different,
uninvolved project maintainer privately through their published GitHub contact
information. Security vulnerabilities use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Moderation

Maintainers responsible for the affected community space act as moderators.
They will consider reports fairly and limit private information to what is
needed to investigate and respond. A person involved in an incident should not
decide the outcome of that report. Not every disagreement is a violation.

Depending on impact, severity, and repeated behavior, moderators may issue a
private warning, restrict specific interactions or activities, temporarily
suspend participation, or permanently ban someone from project spaces. They
may skip steps to protect participants. Restrictions and conditions for return
should be explained to the affected person where it is safe to do so.

Reports and enforcement normally remain private. Public communication should
protect affected people and disclose only what is necessary for community
safety. Retaliation or continued harassment may lead to further restrictions.
